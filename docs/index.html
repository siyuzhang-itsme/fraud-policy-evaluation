Methodology: what these amounts do—and do not—mean
Two different dollar concepts: $543.50 is the naive 1% opportunity cost on all rule-declined legitimate requests. The counterfactual loss estimate comes from separate synthetic randomized arms, not from treating every blocked fraudulent dollar as avoided loss.

Decision guide: retain, refine, retire, investigate or defer

How to interpret each possible payment-rule decision

RETAIN

Keep current rule scope when tested alternatives do not meet guardrails.

REFINE

Narrow the rule when improved selectivity and net outcomes are supported.

RETIRE

Remove the tested layer only when downstream outcomes justify it.

INVESTIGATE NEW RULE

Define and test a new risk hypothesis; no benefit is estimated for untested rules.

DEFER

Keep collecting claims, recoveries and valid labels when evidence or uncertainty is insufficient.

Methods, limitations and evidence status

Provenance & limits: The experimental arms, claim events and calculated rule metrics come from generated fictional inputs. The separately labeled recovery-policy scenario illustrates assumptions, not newly observed chargeback outcomes. No real customer, merchant, employer rule, authorization data or production system is included.

Reproduction: Run python code/generate_demo_inputs.py to generate fictional data/*.csv, then follow the reproducibility commands in README.md. See results/feedback_loop_metrics.json, code/dispute_overlay.py and the tests for details. Author and release citation details appear in CITATION.cff.

Decision methods · Reproducible synthetic comparison

Why evidence maturity changes a decision

Each fictional scenario is evaluated by four methods using the same observed snapshot. Claims-only and gross-loss point estimates omit consumer-access and cost safeguards and consider retirement first. They are deliberately weak illustrative baselines, not representative of all industry methods. The stronger conventional comparator uses access/cost guardrails but treats observed outcomes as complete.

What the stronger comparison demonstrates: On Day 14, the evidence-aware approach defers instead of prematurely retiring a restriction. Once sufficient evidence matures on Day 90, both strong conventional and evidence-aware approaches support narrowing. When labels remain unresolved, the evidence-aware method still defers on Day 90. There is no claim of universal superiority or any live employer performance. results/baseline_comparison.json contains the exact machine-readable decisions.
